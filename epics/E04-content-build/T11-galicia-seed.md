## What this delivers

  `rules.yaml`, `closed_seasons.yaml`, `licence_types.yaml`, `gear_rules.yaml`, `penalties.yaml`,

- `content/shared/` — the species, families, vernacular names, measurement methods, glossary and key
  nodes the Galician rules reference, in all six locales.
- `app/assets/sil/*.svg` — an originated silhouette for every species a Galician rule names.
- `content/CHANGELOG/es-ga.md` and `content/es-ga/snapshot.json` — generated by T09, committed here
  for the first time.

- `content/es-ga/SOURCES.md` — instrument by instrument: what was transcribed, from which DOG or BOE
  URL, on what date, by whom, with the sha256 of the fetched document.

## Why it is built this way

**Galicia, because `SPEC.md` §15 step 3 says Galicia, and the reasons are in §9.1 and §8.** The Xunta
publishes its size tables in Galician, so the seed exercises `gl` — a locale nobody would have tested
by accident. Art. 13 LPI is the one licence basis in the bundle marked **verified**, with a BOE link
in the §8 table, so no content ships against an unresolved provenance question. And Galicia is
Europe's marisqueo heartland, so the seed carries bivalves and crustaceans rather than only finfish,
which is what makes the measurement-method assertions bite: shell length and carapace width are not
total length.

**Every number comes from the instrument, and this task file does not supply one.** The only Galician
value published anywhere in this repository is *Venerupis corrugata* at 38 mm shell length
(`build-assertions.md`). Everything else — the minima, the closure windows, the bag limits — is
transcribed from the DOG and recorded in `content/es-ga/SOURCES.md` with its URL, retrieval date and
digest. A number in the corpus that no line of `SOURCES.md` accounts for is a defect, not a
placeholder, and A9 already refuses a citation whose `source_url` is not on `xunta.gal` or `boe.es`.

**Scale: `SPEC.md` §8 says 100–150 rule rows per jurisdiction, and the seed is allowed to be
smaller.** This task's job is to prove the pipeline end to end and to give E05 a real file to extract;
E22's job is completeness. What the seed must be is **structurally complete** — at least one row in
every table the Galician content model uses, so that no table reaches E05 untested. Case 4 below pins
that as a per-table row count rather than a corpus size, because a corpus size is a number somebody
will meet by duplicating rows.

**The seed carries an expired instrument on purpose.** `resolution-algorithm.md`'s worked hazard is
Galician: the *orde de vedas* is reissued annually and typically lapses on 30 April. Invariant 5 and
`SPEC.md` §7.3 both turn on that case, and E05, E10 and E21 all need real data that exercises it. So
at least one seeded rule carries a `valid_to` in the past relative to the build date, it resolves, and
it is tagged `isExpired`. Case 9 pins it. A seed with only current rules would let the expiry path
reach the field untested.

**`legal_text_locales` records what the Xunta actually published.** `SPEC.md` §7.1 gives
`'gl,es'` as its own example for a bilingual DOG publication, and §9.6 forbids translating legal text.
The seed transcribes the verbatim articles in the language(s) of publication and sets
`legal_text_locales` to match; S13's language-availability notice (E15) is driven by exactly this
value. `content_string` still carries all six locales, because the editorial summaries are ours.

**Zone polygons come from the coordinate annexes to the Orde, and `has_zone_polygons` tells the
truth.** `SPEC.md` §8 marks the Galician polygons as covered by Art. 13 LPI, because the carve-out
reaches the whole *disposición* including its annexes. Where the seed transcribes a coordinate annex,
`zone.min_lat`/`max_lon` and `zone_ring` are filled and `jurisdiction.has_zone_polygons = 1`. Where it
does not, the flag is `0` and S9 hides the sub-zone level (§4.4, §7.1). **We do not invent
boundaries** — the same rule `SPEC.md` §8 states for the Gulf applies to a Galician bank whose
coordinates nobody has transcribed yet.

**Silhouettes are originated SVG and are commissioned as part of this task, not assumed.** `SPEC.md`
§8 puts silhouettes at ~6 MB for ~400 species and marks them ours. A5 fails on a `silhouette_asset`
that names no file, so the seed's species list is bounded by the art that exists. That is the correct
constraint: it is better to ship twenty species with drawings than sixty with broken images, and E22
scales both together.

**Zero cleared plates is the expected outcome, and the build must accept it.** T06 drops any plate
whose illustrator cannot be identified. No Galician plate has been cleared in this repository, so
`plates.yaml` ships empty and every `species.plate_asset` is NULL. §7.1 makes the column nullable and
"optional; cleared per §8" for exactly this reason. A seed that faked a plate to make the ledger look
populated would be the "pending state that ships" rule 8 forbids.

**The end-to-end test runs the real CLI, not a library call.** `catchlaw-content-pipeline` rule 1 says
the tool is a deliverable; a test that bypasses `bin/build.dart` proves the library and not the thing
CI runs. The test invokes the CLI against `content/`, asserts exit 0, then opens the emitted database
**read-only** and asserts against it.

## Implementation outline

1. **Transcribe first, author second.** Open the DOG and BOE sources named in `SPEC.md` §8, record
   each instrument in `content/es-ga/SOURCES.md` with its URL, retrieval date, sha256 and the human
   who read it, then write `citations.yaml` from that file. A citation authored before its
   `SOURCES.md` line exists will fail A4 or A9 and should.
2. `jurisdiction.yaml`: `code: ES-GA`, `country_iso2: ES`, `default_locale: gl`,
   `legal_text_locales` matching what the DOG actually published, `has_saltwater: 1`,
   `has_zone_polygons` set honestly, `content_version`, `published_on`, `checked_on`.
3. `zones.yaml`: at least one `region`, one `subzone` and one `bank` with a real parent chain, so the
   specificity ladder and E11's ancestry query have something to stand on. Coordinate annexes
   transcribed where they exist; `has_zone_polygons = 0` where they do not.
4. `content/shared/species.yaml` and `vernacular.yaml`: the species the Galician rules name, with all
   six locales. `gl`, `es` and `ca` names from the instruments; `en` from the Catalogue of Life
   vernacular extension and from nowhere else (`SPEC.md` §9.2 step 2); `ar` and `pt_BR` from CoL where
   they exist and a `no_vernacular` declaration where they do not (T05).
5. Commission and commit one originated silhouette per species.
6. `rules.yaml`: the transcribed rows, every size with its method, every closure with its window, one
   row deliberately carrying a past `valid_to`.
7. `legal_text.yaml`: the verbatim articles, single-locale, in the language of publication.
8. `strings.yaml` in both `shared/` and `es-ga/`: every `*_key` in all six locales.
9. `changes.yaml`: the initial entry, `from_version` empty, `to_version` the seed's version.
10. Run the build; work the failure list down to zero. Do not disable an assertion; if one is wrong,
    fix the assertion in its own commit and say so.
11. Commit the generated `snapshot.json`, `CHANGELOG/es-ga.md`, `reference.db.gz` and
    `reference.build.json`.

## Definition of done

`CONVENTIONS.md` §8 applies in full, plus:

- [ ] All 17 rows pass, and each failed first.
- [ ] `dart run content_builder:build --in content/ --out app/assets/db/reference.db --build-date
      <date> --generator-commit <sha>` exits 0, and all ten assertion ids appear in the summary.
- [ ] Removing any single required field from any single Galician row makes the build exit 1 and
      write nothing — spot-checked against at least three different assertions.
- [ ] Every citation id in the corpus has a line in `content/es-ga/SOURCES.md` naming the DOG or BOE
      URL, the retrieval date, the human who read it and the document's sha256.
- [ ] No numeric value in the corpus is unaccounted for by `SOURCES.md`.
- [ ] `app/assets/db/reference.build.json` records the sha256 of the uncompressed file, and two
      consecutive builds agree.
- [ ] `content/CHANGELOG/es-ga.md` and `content/es-ga/snapshot.json` are committed and `--check`
      exits 0.
- [ ] `plates.yaml` is empty and every `species.plate_asset` is NULL.
- [ ] The corpus contains at least one rule expired at the build date, and it still resolves.
- [ ] `check_content_pipeline.sh content` clean.
