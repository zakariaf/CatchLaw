# IDEAS.md — Phase 2 Longlist

24 candidates (the brief asked for 20; four extra survived the cut and are worth recording).
Format: **Name** — who it's for — what it does — why offline — evidence strength — best source link.

Evidence strength is judged against the brief's bar: **validated = ≥3 independent sources across
≥2 platforms, from real users.** Anything below that is labelled honestly.

> **Read the environment caveat first:** reddit.com is unreachable from this environment, so no Reddit
> evidence exists anywhere in this research. Substitute primary sources were used throughout
> (Discourse JSON APIs, Stack Exchange API, HN Algolia, Apple App Store review pages and review RSS,
> the GitHub REST API, F-Droid, eCFR, Google Play storefront search, regulator documents). Details in
> `research/my-verification-notes.md`.

## Speculative section — liked, but not evidenced, and therefore **not eligible to win**

Per the brief these are quarantined. Each is an idea I find genuinely attractive but could not validate
to the ≥3-sources-across-≥2-platforms bar.

- **Offline radio-telemetry triangulation** (wildlife trackers) — bearings → maximum-likelihood fix + error
  ellipse on the ridgeline, so you know which drainage to walk into *now* instead of a week later. Needs no
  bundled data at all (pure geodesy + the public-domain WMM/IGRF coefficients). The desktop incumbent (LOAS) is
  Windows post-processing. **No community, no size, no complaint found.**
- **Offline avalanche-control shot and misfire log** — legally required same-day under CA Title 8 §5357, recorded
  on a ridge in a storm in gloves before the lifts open, with dud locations carried to tomorrow's route card.
  Excellent offline case, public-domain regulation. **No practitioner complaint and no membership number retrievable.**
- **Offline field-archaeology single-context recording + live Harris matrix** — a genuinely hard, genuinely useful
  build (cycle detection, automatic stratigraphic layout), with Historic England's manual under the Open
  Government Licence. **Existing offline products (Kiosk, FAIMS, iDAI.field) make the gap narrower than it looks.**
- **Offline letterpress press and metal-type identification key** — 9,933 posts filed under "Press and typeface
  identification" on Briar Press, and pre-1930 foundry specimen books are public domain. **But the honest offline
  moment is only "identifying a press in a barn before you commit to hauling it", which is thin.**
- **Offline gundog field-trial judge's sheet and training diary** — 700+ Royal Kennel Club trials a year, effectively
  zero apps. **No handler or judge complaint found.**
- **Offline aviation ramp-agent reference** — an iTunes sweep found **zero** ramp-facing tools of any kind, which is
  striking. **Not investigated further; budget ran out.**
- **Offline blacksmith/bladesmith shop reference** — spark-test identification key, forging-colour chart, developed-length
  math. **Demoted, not merely speculative: the useful heat-treat dataset is ASM's ~$250 copyrighted guide, and there
  is no public-domain equivalent.**
