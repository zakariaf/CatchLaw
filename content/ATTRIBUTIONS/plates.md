# Bundled plates

Generated by `dart run content_builder:build`. Do not edit by hand.

Every plate is cleared on its illustrator's **death year**, against the longest term among the jurisdictions this app ships into — Spain's 80 years *post mortem auctoris* for authors who died before 7 December 1987. Publication year is evidence about the artist and is never the test.

No plates are bundled.
