Sounder — the app is equipment

Not an app that happens to run at sea. An instrument that belongs on the console next to the chartplotter and the echo sounder: bezelled panels, hairline rules, silkscreen labels, tabular numerals, every value a readout. Dark-committed because it is used at 05:40 in the dark, and a white screen at that hour costs you your night vision and ten seconds you do not have.

SECTION A

The core loop

Fish in the bin, hands wet, no signal, ten seconds. Species in, statement of fact out — with the number that decided it, how that number is measured, and where it comes from.

SECTION B

Finding the fish

Three ways in, because a name you cannot spell is no way in at all. Species live under their local names — هامور, not Epinephelus coioides. The binomial is the small grey line underneath.

SECTION C

Log and reference

The tally is a working document, not a diary — it exists so the next check knows how many you already have. The reference shelf is the receipts: the instruments themselves, in the language they were written in.

Where the direction earns its keep: a conflict it refuses to resolve for you, a third theme for full sun, a glove layout, and the one-time unpack on first launch.

Why this direction

Khalid is not opening an app; he is reading an instrument, in the dark, with one hand, while a fish is still alive in the bin. So Sounder borrows its whole visual grammar from the hardware already bolted to his console — bezelled panels with visible fasteners, hairline rules, silkscreened micro-labels, and monospaced tabular numerals that hold their column whether the value is 7 or 3,000. Committing to a dark blue-green ground is not a style preference: a white screen at 05:40 costs him his night vision, and the sodium amber accent is the colour of every harbour lamp and instrument backlight he already trusts, which is exactly why it is reserved for chrome and never allowed to state a result. The measurement numeral is set enormous with the unit and the method code as small superior labels beside it, because at arm’s length in the dark the only thing that must survive is the number and whether it cleared the line — the words and the icon carry the verdict so the colour never has to work alone. Everything else on the result panel exists to make the statement defensible rather than persuasive: the method diagram, the instrument and its two dates, and a disclaimer that cannot be dismissed because a legal-liability constraint is not a piece of UI to be tidied away. And when two rules collide, the panel does what a real instrument does with a contradictory reading — it shows both traces and refuses to average them.

Note on theming: the page chrome around these frames follows your light/dark preference and your toggle. The screens inside them do not — the app ships one committed instrument palette plus a separate sunlight mode, and showing it inverted would misrepresent the product.
